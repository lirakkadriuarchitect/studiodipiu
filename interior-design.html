Bar & Restaurant

Tintin Cocktail Bar

01 / 11
bar & restaurant

plaster · brass
aged timber
local stone · leather

97 m²
The bar was designed from the surface in. Texture precedes form — raw plaster absorbs candlelight rather than deflecting it. Every angle was resolved to resist hierarchy: no seat is more privileged than another, no corner reduced to mere circulation. The space earns its stillness.

Bar & Lounge

Late Night Bar

01 / 09
bar & lounge

concrete · steel
smoked glass
oxidised metal · linen

concept
A room that exists only after dark. Geometry was resolved before any palette was considered — structure first, atmosphere as consequence. Dark materials were chosen not for drama but for their capacity to absorb presence, letting people and light become the only points of definition.
